## Publication 2 — ICUC12 Conference Paper (2025)

**Title:** How Does Urban Design and Heat Influence Our Cycling Choices?  
**Venue:** 12th International Conference on Urban Climate (ICUC12), Rotterdam  
**Abstract:** https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/ICUC12/ICUC12-769.html

**What it shows:**
- Analysed cycling count/speed data in Brussels correlated with thermal indices (PET, UTCI, Mean Radiant Temperature) and green infrastructure pixel-level analysis
- Key finding: greener areas increase cycling activity during warm months, especially weekends/mornings; above PET > 35°C the greenness benefit diminishes
- Policy-relevant: informs where to prioritize tree planting and shade infrastructure to maintain cycling under climate change

**Hero metric:** Demonstrated quantifiable relationship between greenness, thermal thresholds, and cycling behavior — directly actionable for urban planners

**Use when applying to:** Urban Analytics, Smart Mobility, Sustainability Tech, City Planning roles
